# Privacy Policy for Today Prep: Study Focus Timer

**Effective Date: September 30, 2026**

Today Prep: Study Focus Timer ("Today Prep", "we", "our", or "the App") is a study planning and focus application developed under **AppStudio RB**.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Today Prep.

## 1. Information We Store

Today Prep is designed so that your personal study information is primarily stored on your device.

This may include:

- Study schedules and timetable entries
- Study topics, subjects, and chapter information
- Task completion and study progress
- Focus-session settings and related app preferences
- Information you enter into the app for planning and study purposes

We do not operate a cloud database for your personal study timetable or study progress through the App.

## 2. Account Information

Today Prep does not require you to create an account, provide a phone number, or provide a password in order to use the main study-planning and focus features.

## 3. Device Features and Permissions

Depending on the version and features you use, Today Prep may use Android device capabilities such as:

### Notifications and Alarms
Used to provide scheduled study reminders, alerts, and focus-session notifications.

### Vibration and Audio
Used to notify you when a scheduled study session or reminder starts.

### Full-Screen Study Alerts
Used for important study reminders when a scheduled session begins, where permitted by your device settings.

### Focus Mode
Certain Focus Mode features may use Android system capabilities to help reduce distractions while you are studying.

The App does not use these features to record your keystrokes, private messages, or other personal communications.

## 4. Advertising

Today Prep may use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and other information permitted under Google's advertising technologies and policies for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization where applicable.

You can manage advertising and personalization settings through your Android device and your Google account settings.

Google's privacy information is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Google Play Billing and Lifetime PRO

Today Prep may offer a **Lifetime PRO** purchase through Google Play Billing.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card, debit card, UPI, or other payment credentials.

The App may receive purchase and entitlement information from Google Play to determine whether Lifetime PRO access should be enabled and restored on your device.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Study information stored locally by the App remains on your device until you remove it or clear the App's data.

You may delete locally stored App data by using the device's app-data controls, clearing the relevant app data, or uninstalling the App, subject to your device's normal operating-system behavior.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google is subject to those services' own policies and retention practices.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Today Prep is intended for students and learners.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary collection of personal information.

Because study-planning information is primarily stored locally on your device, you should protect your device using appropriate security features such as a screen lock and operating-system security updates.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services, including Google services such as Google AdMob and Google Play Billing.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised Effective Date.
